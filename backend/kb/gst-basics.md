# GST basics (general information, not tax advice)

GSTIN is the 15-character GST identification number given to a registered business.

For a sale within the same state, GST is split into CGST (central) and SGST or UTGST (state or union territory). For a sale between states, a single IGST is charged instead.

Taxable value is the price of the goods or services before GST is added. Invoice value is the taxable value plus the tax.

Input tax credit (ITC) lets a registered business reduce the GST it pays by the GST it already paid on eligible purchases used for the business.

GSTR-1 is the return in which a registered business reports its outward sales. GSTR-2B is an auto-generated statement of the input tax credit available from suppliers' filings. Return types, due dates and rules change, so always confirm current requirements with the GST portal or a chartered accountant.

Aethra reads GST-style sales files by looking for columns such as taxable value, invoice date and the tax columns. It uses taxable value as the sales amount.
